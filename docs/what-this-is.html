The north star

Three promises. Everything is measured against them.

01 · One place
Your whole life behind one surface, one grant.

Not N apps with N logins and N configs. You connect the sectors of your life once, and from then on it's a single thing to point an agent at.

02 · Lightning fast
It answers at the speed of memory, not the speed of the network.

Reads come straight from a fresh local copy in milliseconds — we never wait on a dozen far-off services while you sit there. Acting is just as quick: the slow part of "do something" has always been figuring out what to act on, and we already know that from the local copy, so all that's left is the one unavoidable command going out. No tax on top.

03 · Harness-agnostic
Swap the agent. Keep the context, the reach, the permission, the record.

What one agent could see and do, the next one can too — the moment you point it here. Nothing to reconnect, nothing left behind.

If a decision weakens one of these, it's the wrong decision.

The one thing that makes it different

Swap the harness. Keep the context.

Harness portability

Today, your context lives inside whatever assistant you happen to use. When you leave, it stays behind. Here it's the other way around: the context is yours, and the agent is the guest.

Move from Claude, to a model running on your own machine, to whatever comes next — the same connected context surface is available, and the new agent can query it immediately. You change harnesses without ever reconnecting every account.
